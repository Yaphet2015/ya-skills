## F14 — bounded, abortable supervisor lock ownership

**Implementation evidence**

- `acquireSupervisorLock` now has a finite deadline and an abort-aware sleep loop. It runs before `superviseOnce` creates the main run deadline.
- Missing, malformed, and structurally invalid `owner.json` records produce `SupervisorLockError.code === "owner_identity_unknown"` at the bounded deadline. A live owner plus an external abort produces `supervisor_lock_aborted`.
- Releasing a lock rechecks the acquisition PID/token. Unknown or replaced ownership is preserved; uncertain acquisition never recursively deletes the other lane's lock. Dead-owner reclamation serializes through a private marker and rechecks the recorded PID/token before removing it.
- `supervisorLockPath` and `supervisorLockTimeoutMs` are internal test seams only; production uses the fixed private temporary lock and a bounded default.

**Regression evidence**

- `tests/computer-lane-e2e-supervisor.test.ts` covers missing, parse-corrupt, and structurally invalid owner records; each finishes within the private 40ms bound, reports uncertainty, leaves the lock present, and creates no run directory.
- The abort case uses a private lock naming the current process as another owner, aborts during acquisition, and verifies both the lock directory and owner record remain.

## P2 — incremental E2E spool reader

**Implementation evidence**

- `IncrementalE2ESpoolReader` replaces the supervisor's growing-file `readFileSync` on every 20ms tick with one bounded `readSync` per poll (64KiB default), retaining an absolute byte offset.
- It delegates byte-count and fatal streaming UTF-8 framing to `FrameReader`, tracks an unterminated tail, and reports truncation/rotation rather than concatenating old and new frames or accepting an incomplete final event.
- Supervisor shutdown drains one final bounded poll, rejects a non-newline tail as a worker protocol error, closes the reader FD, and only then removes the private spool.

**Regression evidence**

- The lane spool tests prove appended data is read from the prior offset, split UTF-8 survives one-byte reads, a partial tail is incomplete, in-place truncation and inode replacement are explicit errors, malformed UTF-8 fails, and a frame beyond `MAX_MESSAGE_BYTES` fails.
- Every spool fixture is private and removed by the test; no process sweep or global PID kill is used.
